Build Your List
A list in Apollo.io is your curated audience for a specific campaign. Lists can be added directly to sequences or workflows, enriched, or exported. Think of it as the foundation — everything else depends on list quality.
1.1
Define your ICP first BEFORE APOLLO
Know who you're targeting before touching any filter
🏢
Define your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — before opening Apollo, know your target: industry, company size, seniority level, geography, and the pain points your product solves. This prevents wasted credits and unfocused outreach.
Key ICP attributes to define: Industry · Company size (headcount/revenue) · Seniority (VP, Director, C-suite) · Geography · Technologies used · Pain points
↗ Create and Use a List — knowledge.apollo.io
1.2
Use People filters strategically HIGH IMPACT
Layered filters = better conversion
🔍
Go to Search > People and apply layered filters. The more specific your combination, the higher your conversion rate. Don't rely on just one filter.
⚡
Priority filters to combine: Buying Intent (High/Medium), Job Title, Seniority, Company Size, Location, and Technologies used. Buying Intent is the strongest signal — always include it.
↗ People Filters Guide — knowledge.apollo.io
1.3
Name lists clearly & enrich before enrolling
Reusability + verified data = protected sender reputation
📝
Use descriptive list names like "SaaS CFOs US Q2 2026" so the list is reusable across sequences and workflows. Avoid generic names like "List 1" — you'll lose track fast.
✅
Enrich before enrolling — use Apollo's enrichment to verify emails and phone numbers before adding contacts to any sequence. Unverified emails hurt your sender reputation and deliverability.
💳
Watch your credits — 1 email credit per verified email for net-new contacts. Adding previously saved contacts to a list is free. Enrich only who you actually plan to contact.
↗ Create and Use a List — knowledge.apollo.io

Create Your Sequence
A sequence is an automated, multi-step outreach campaign that chains emails, phone call tasks, LinkedIn tasks, and other actions over a defined schedule — keeping you in front of prospects without manual follow-up.
2.1
Pick the right creation method START HERE
AI, template, or scratch — choose based on your need for control
🤖
AI-assisted — fastest option, single-click. Best when you need to move quickly or you're testing a new market segment.
📋
Template — proven frameworks for common outbound scenarios. Best for standard ICP campaigns where you want a reliable starting point.
⚙️
Build from scratch — use only when you need full control over every step, timing, and messaging. Higher effort, highest customization.
↗ Sequences Overview — knowledge.apollo.io
2.2
Go omnichannel HIGH IMPACT
Multichannel sequences outperform single-channel every time
🔀
Apollo strongly recommends combining channels — email, phone call tasks, and LinkedIn steps together consistently outperform single-channel sequences.

Day 3 → 📞 Call
Day 5 → 📧 Email

2.3
Schedule, rulesets & verified contacts only
Protect your domain and ensure deliverability
🕐
Set a sending schedule — configure the days and times emails go out. Avoid weekends and holidays. Business hours in the prospect's timezone dramatically improve open rates.
🛡️
Configure a ruleset — create a sequence ruleset to automatically stop sending to prospects who reply, bounce, opt out, or meet other criteria. This protects your domain reputation.
✅
Only add verified contacts — Apollo warns you before adding contacts with unverified emails. Never skip this warning. Bounced emails damage your sender score and reduce deliverability for the entire team.

Build Your Workflow
Workflows are Apollo's automation engine. They remove manual steps by automatically enrolling the right contacts into the right sequences at the right time — based on triggers like schedule, buying intent signals, job changes, or CRM field updates.
3.1
Start with AI or a template QUICK START
Skip blank-page paralysis — let Apollo build the structure
🤖
Use the AI assistant — describe your goal in plain English, e.g. "Find high-intent SaaS leads and add them to our outbound sequence." It builds the workflow and asks for confirmation before activating.
📋
Or pick a template — Apollo's library covers the most common use cases. Templates come pre-wired with triggers and filters you can adapt in minutes. Build from scratch only when you need full control over every block.
↗ Workflows Overview — knowledge.apollo.io
3.2
Set the right trigger CRITICAL
Match the trigger type to the business event
📅
Schedule-based triggers (daily/weekly) — best for prospecting workflows. A fresh batch of matching leads enters on a set cadence.
🔄
Field-based triggers (CRM field update) — best for renewals, job changes, or intent spikes. The workflow fires the moment the triggering event is detected.
Rule: Mismatching trigger type to use case is the #1 workflow setup mistake. Always ask: is this event-driven or time-driven?
↗ Workflows Overview — knowledge.apollo.io
3.3
Enrollment filters + multi-split branches HIGH IMPACT
Right people → right sequence → right mailbox
🎯
Tight enrollment filters — apply Buying Intent (High/Medium), persona, seniority, and company size. Tight filters = higher quality leads. Loose filters = wasted credits and low reply rates.
🌿
Multi-split branches by persona — add a Multi-split branch block and create one branch per persona (e.g. VP Sales, CFO, Founder). Persona-specific messaging dramatically improves reply rates.
📬
Dedicated mailbox per branch — add a "Manage Sequences" block to each branch. Assign a specific mailbox — never "rotate all." This ensures replies land in one inbox per persona and nothing gets missed.
↗ Workflows Overview — knowledge.apollo.io
3.4
Re-enrollment rules & team sharing
Prevent over-contacting. Scale what works.
⏱️
Re-enrollment = once per 90 days — the safe default for most prospecting workflows. Prevents over-contacting the same prospect and protects your domain reputation.
👥
Share with your team — once performing well, share internally so teammates can clone and adapt it. You can also publish as a public template via LinkedIn or X.
↗ Workflows Overview — knowledge.apollo.io

Website Visitors
Apollo's Website Visitor tracking lets you identify which companies and contacts are visiting your site in real time. Install a tracking snippet, uncover hidden demand, and automatically route high-intent visitors into sequences or workflows — before they ever fill out a form.
4.1
Install the tracking snippet SETUP FIRST
The script goes inside <head> tags — not body
⚙️
Go to Settings → Prospecting → Website Visitors — add your domain and copy the Apollo tracking snippet. All plans support up to 3 domains. Need more? Get the Inbound add-on to track up to 100 domains.
📍
Place the snippet inside <head> tags — not in the body. To verify, use Chrome DevTools → Sources tab and confirm assets.apollo.io appears. A status code of 204 on the Network tab confirms successful tracking.
🍪
Cookie consent compliance — if your site uses a consent management platform (CMP), configure it so the Apollo script only fires after consent is granted. Classify it under Analytics or Marketing in your CMP settings.
Note: Website visitor data may take up to 7 days to appear in your account. Contact-level tracking is available only for U.S.-based contacts. International visitors are tracked at the company level only.
↗ Track Website Visitors — knowledge.apollo.io
4.2
Label high-intent pages & filter visitors HIGH IMPACT
Pricing and demo pages signal buying intent — prioritize them
🏷️
Label pages as High Intent or Low Intent — pricing pages, demo pages, and product pages signal buying readiness. Blog or SEO pages don't. Only meaningful traffic should trigger outbound actions.
🔍
Use filters to focus on your ICP — Apollo automatically filters visitors from the past 90 days. Refine by time period, intent level, visited URLs, or to show net-new contacts only. Save your search and set up alerts for new matching records.
📊
Use analytics to spot trends — go to Website Visitors → Analytics to see top 10 visiting companies, top pages viewed, visitor breakdown by industry, location, company size, and revenue segment.
↗ Use Website Visitors Data — knowledge.apollo.io
4.3
Automate outreach with a Website Visited workflow AUTOMATE
Turn anonymous visits into pipeline automatically
⚡
Use the "Website Visited" workflow trigger — create a workflow that fires when an account visits a tracked page. Select your domain, set intent filters (High/Medium), and connect it to your best outbound sequence.
🔔
Send Slack or email alerts — notify the account owner the moment a high-intent prospect visits your site so your team can act quickly, before the prospect loses momentum.
📋
Auto-add to a "Golden Intent Leads" list — apply ICP filters and automatically save matching visitors to a dedicated list for organized, prioritized outreach.
  • Snippet installed & connection verified (status 204)
  • High-intent pages labeled in Apollo
  • "Website Visited" workflow created with ICP filters
  • Slack/email alerts set up for account owners
↗ Use Website Visitors Data — knowledge.apollo.io

Forms & Form Enrichment
Apollo's Form Enrichment keeps your web forms short and frictionless by auto-filling fields with Apollo data — increasing conversions while giving you richer lead data. Connect forms to Inbound Routers to automatically route and schedule meetings with qualified leads.
5.1
Keep forms short with auto-fill & form shortening CONVERSION
Long forms kill conversions — Apollo fills the rest automatically
✨
Enable Auto-fill — when a prospect enters their email, Apollo automatically populates known fields (company name, size, location, title) from its database. This reduces friction and increases submission rates.
🙈
Enable Form Shortening (Hide auto-filled fields) — prospects only see the fields they still need to complete. Shorter visible forms = dramatically higher conversion rates.
✅
More accurate data, zero extra effort — Apollo auto-fills even optional fields, so you get complete, accurate records without asking more of the prospect. Form enrichment credits are free for all Apollo users.
Platform-agnostic: Form enrichment works with Marketo, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Webflow, Wix, and any custom HTML form.
↗ Use Form Enrichment — knowledge.apollo.io
5.2
Set up Inbound Routers to auto-route leads CRITICAL
Route every lead to the right rep — automatically
🗺️
Create an Inbound Router before the form — Inbound Routers automate meeting scheduling and lead distribution. You need a router set up before integrating with your web form. Go to Meetings → Create → Inbound Router Setup.
🌿
Use If/Else rules to route by ICP criteria — create branching logic based on company size, industry, territory, or intent. For example: 5000+ employees → Enterprise rep; everyone else → SMB team. Prospects always land with the right person.
🔗
Map form fields exactly — field names in Apollo must match your HTML form field names exactly (case-sensitive). If your form uses emailaddress, don't type email in Apollo. Mismatched fields break routing.
↗ Create Inbound Routers — knowledge.apollo.io
5.3
Trigger workflows from form submissions AUTOMATE
Form submitted → instant, automated follow-up
⚡
Use "Form Submitted" as a workflow trigger — instantly automate what happens after a lead submits a form. No manual handoff, no delays. The workflow fires the moment the submission is received.
🤝
Auto-create & enrich contacts — when a new prospect submits your form, Apollo automatically creates a contact, enriches their record, and pushes the data to your CRM. Existing contacts get their records updated with net-new data.
📋
Reuse intake form templates — once you create a form template, reuse it across multiple inbound routers. You can also use form submissions and abandoned forms as separate workflow triggers for follow-up.
  • Form enrichment enabled on your domain
  • Auto-fill & form shortening toggled on
  • Inbound Router created with If/Else routing rules
  • Form fields mapped exactly (case-sensitive)
  • "Form Submitted" workflow trigger set up

6.2
Domain health & deliverability checks
A broken domain kills your entire team's outreach
🩺
Check sequence diagnostics regularly — Apollo's diagnostics surface domain health and deliverability issues before they become serious problems.
⚠️
Warning signs to watch for: rising bounce rates, drop in open rates across sequences, replies landing in spam. Any one of these signals a domain reputation issue needing immediate attention.
  • Check sequence diagnostics weekly
  • Monitor bounce rate (keep under 2%)
  • Review ruleset — ensure it catches bounces & replies
  • Validate new contacts before enrolling
↗ Sequence Diagnostics — knowledge.apollo.io
6.3
The iteration loop
How to improve when performance drops
🔁
Don't tweak — rebuild — when a sequence consistently falls below benchmarks, create a completely new variant with a different angle, subject line, and opening. Small tweaks rarely produce meaningful lifts.
📐
Test one variable at a time — when running A/B tests, isolate the variable (subject line only, or body only). This gives you clean data on what actually moved performance.
🏆
Share winning sequences — once a sequence hits all three benchmarks consistently, share it with your team so everyone benefits. What works for one persona often works across similar roles.
